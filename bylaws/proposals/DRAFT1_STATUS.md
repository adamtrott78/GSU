## Assumptions incorporated into Draft 1

- Local 6350 keeps its existing name in the draft.
- The graduate bargaining unit is called **Graduate Student Employees**.
- GSU elects President, Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary.
- The elected GSU President serves as the principal liaison to Local 6350 leadership; no new Local-wide officer or Executive Board seat is created.
- GSU officers serve one-year terms.
- GSU office is exempt from the existing one-year prior-membership requirement.
- GSU has a Representative Council separate from the Local Executive Board.
- Council representation is primarily by academic college/school, with two at-large seats.
- RA/TA, Master's/PhD, international-worker, and similar overlapping interests are handled through committees/caucuses rather than overlapping electoral districts.
- GSU selects its own bargaining committee.
- GSU members alone vote on ratification of the GSU contract.
- meaningful mid-term vacancies are normally filled by special election.
- A temporary gap between covered graduate appointments, including a summer without a covered appointment, does not by itself terminate GSU membership or elected-office eligibility when the member is expected to return to a covered appointment in the immediately following academic term.
- AFT-required democratic-election safeguards are incorporated into the election provisions.
- the clarified full / half / quarter / eighth dues framework is incorporated.
- Article XIII's existing fifteen-day distribution / two-thirds adoption process remains the constitutional amendment mechanism.

## Leadership decisions for September 22

Leadership is being asked to approve, modify, or reject the concrete proposals in `TUESDAY_DECISION_SHEET.md`.

The highest-value discussion points are:

1. **Officer architecture:** four GSU officers, with the GSU President serving as the principal liaison to existing Local leadership.
2. **Terms and eligibility:** one-year terms and no one-year waiting period.
3. **Representative Council:** separate from the Local Executive Board; college/school representation; two at-large seats; proposed representation thresholds.
4. **Cross-cutting representation:** committees/caucuses rather than RA/TA or MS/PhD electoral districts.
5. **Bargaining and ratification:** GSU selects its bargaining team and participates directly in administration of its own contract-ratification process in coordination with Local officers.
6. **Vacancies and transition:** special-election rules and the initial post-amendment election.
7. **Temporary appointment gaps:** confirm the proposed summer/appointment-gap continuity rule.
8. **Optional Local-name discussion:** Draft 1 keeps the current name, but leadership may discuss whether a broader umbrella name should eventually be recommended.

## Existing Local structures intentionally preserved

Draft 1 does **not** attempt to redesign unrelated Maintainers governance.

It preserves:
- Local President, Vice-President, Treasurer, and Secretary;
- existing Local-wide committee-chair seats on the Executive Board;
- the chief labor-council delegate seat;
- the Maintainers bargaining committee structure, except for clarifying that it is the Maintainers bargaining committee rather than the universal committee for all units;
- Local treasury control and existing financial safeguards.
