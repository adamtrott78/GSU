# TUESDAY MEETING BRIEF — GSU GOVERNANCE PROPOSAL

**Meeting date:** September 22, 2026  
**Goal:** Leave the meeting with leadership approval in principle—or a precise list of changes—so the constitutional draft can go to the Local 6350 President for review.

---

# 1. Opening — approximately 1 minute

### Core point

We do not need to invent a union structure from scratch.

Local 6350's existing Constitution already:
- recognizes chapter-specific voting language;
- restricts contract ratification to the affected bargaining unit.

Chelsea Local 1340 and Chelmsford Local 3569 show two established AFT approaches to multiple bargaining units.

The proposal combines the parts that fit graduate workers best.

### One-sentence proposal

> Keep Local 6350 as the umbrella, make GSU a democratically governed bargaining-unit chapter, give the elected GSU President a guaranteed Local Executive Board seat, and create a separate GSU Representative Council for broad graduate-worker representation.

LOCAL 6350

    ├── college/school representatives
    ├── 2 at-large representatives

        ├── Grievance / Contract
        └── Organizing / Communications

### Emphasize

The Representative Council is **not** automatically the Local Executive Board.

That keeps broad GSU representation without letting the size of the GSU council accidentally determine Local-wide voting balance.

---

# 3. Explain why this model — approximately 2 minutes

## Chelsea lesson

Chelsea has bargaining-unit-specific vice presidents who:
- sit on the Executive Board;
- communicate with their units;
- participate in negotiations;
- participate in grievances.

## Chelmsford lesson

Chelmsford has:
- one Bargaining Unit Vice President per bargaining unit;
- a Representative Council;
- the Representative Council separate from the Executive Board;
- representatives elected by worksite;
- unit-specific bargaining teams.

## Why adapt rather than copy

Graduate workers have:
- shorter employment cycles;
- shifting RA/TA assignments;
- overlapping Master's/PhD, research/teaching, and international-worker identities.

So the proposal uses:
- one-year terms;
- no one-year waiting period for GSU office;
- college/school electoral constituencies;
- committees for overlapping identities.

# 4. Walk through the decisions — approximately 4 minutes

Use `TUESDAY_DECISION_SHEET.md`.

Do not reopen the whole structure after every item.

Present the recommendation first, then ask whether anyone has an objection or modification.

The decisions are:

1. four elected GSU officers;
2. GSU President also holds the Local GSU seat;
3. one-year terms;
4. no one-year waiting period for GSU office;
5. separate GSU Representative Council;
6. hybrid college/school seat formula;
7. RA/TA and MS/PhD handled through committees/caucuses;
8. GSU controls selection of its bargaining team;
9. special elections for meaningful vacancies;
10. initial transition election after the amendments take effect.

Record changes directly on the decision sheet.

---

# 5. Close — approximately 1 minute

### Requested action

Ask leadership to approve a motion or consensus statement substantially like:

> GSU leadership approves the proposed governance structure in principle, subject to the modifications recorded during this meeting, and authorizes the revised draft Local 6350 constitutional amendments and GSU chapter bylaws to be provided to the Local 6350 President for review.

### Make clear what leadership is NOT doing Tuesday

Leadership is not:
- adopting the Local Constitution;
- bypassing the Local membership;
- declaring the bylaws final;
- deciding AFT compliance questions without review.

Leadership is approving the **proposal to advance for formal Local review**.

---

# 6. Questions to park for the Local President / AFT

If these questions threaten to consume the meeting, record them and move on:

- Does the Local's legal/chartered name need to change?
- Is "Graduate Student Vice-President" the preferred AFT title?
- What exact election notice is required?
- Does AFT require a minimum eligibility period for the GSU President's Local seat?
- How should the existing Maintainers-based dues formula be handled?
- Does AFT/AFT Massachusetts need to approve anything before the member vote?

These matter before final circulation, but they do not prevent GSU leadership from deciding what structure it wants.

---

# 7. Desired end state

At the end of the meeting, there should be:

- one approved-in-principle governance model;
- marked decisions on the decision sheet;
- a short list of requested edits;
- authorization to revise the drafts;
- authorization to give the revised package to the Local 6350 President.

That is enough to move immediately into formal review.
