# PROPOSED BYLAWS OF THE GRADUATE STUDENT EMPLOYEES CHAPTER (GSU), LOCAL 6350

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT FOR LEADERSHIP REVIEW — NOT ADOPTED  
**Prepared:** September 2026  
**Parent organization:** U-Mass Dartmouth Maintainers, Local 6350, AFT Massachusetts, AFL-CIO

These proposed chapter bylaws are designed to operate only if the Local 6350 Constitution is amended to recognize the Graduate Student Employees Chapter and authorize chapter bylaws. If there is a conflict, the Local 6350 Constitution, applicable law, governing documents of affiliated organizations, and the collective bargaining agreement control.

---

# ARTICLE I — NAME AND STATUS

## Section 1. Name

The organization governed by these bylaws shall be known as the **Graduate Student Union (GSU)** and shall constitute the **Graduate Student Employees Chapter of Local 6350**.

## Section 2. Status

GSU is a bargaining-unit chapter of Local 6350 and is not a separate AFT local.

## Section 3. Purpose

GSU exists to democratically represent Graduate Student Employees; protect and enforce their collective bargaining rights; organize members; develop bargaining priorities; support members in workplace disputes; communicate with the bargaining unit; and participate fully in Local 6350.

---

# ARTICLE II — MEMBERSHIP

## Section 1. Membership

Membership consists of members in good standing of Local 6350 who are represented in the Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit.

A temporary gap between covered Graduate Student Employees appointments, including a summer period without a covered appointment, does not by itself terminate GSU membership when the member is expected to return to a covered Graduate Student Employees appointment in the immediately following academic term.

## Section 2. Rights of members

Each member in good standing has the right to:

a. attend GSU membership meetings;  
b. speak and make motions subject to meeting rules;  
c. vote in GSU-wide elections and membership votes;  
d. vote for representatives assigned to the member's electoral constituency;  
e. run for GSU office or Representative Council subject to these bylaws;  
f. participate in committees and caucuses for which the member is eligible;  
g. receive reasonable access to these bylaws, approved meeting minutes, election results, and other non-confidential GSU governance records; and  
h. petition GSU leadership as provided by these bylaws.

## Section 3. Bargaining-unit status

Nothing in these bylaws expands or contracts the legally recognized Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit. Questions regarding bargaining-unit inclusion shall be governed by applicable recognition documents, collective bargaining agreements, and law.

---

# ARTICLE III — OFFICERS

## Section 1. Officers

GSU shall elect:

a. President  
b. Vice President  
c. Treasurer  
d. Secretary

## Section 2. Terms

Officers shall serve one-year terms beginning July 1 and ending June 30, except for the initial transition term established in Article XV.

Officers may seek reelection while otherwise eligible.

## Section 3. Eligibility

Any GSU member in good standing is eligible to seek and hold GSU office.

A temporary appointment gap described in Article II, Section 1 does not by itself make an elected officer or Representative Council member ineligible to complete the term while the member remains expected to return to a covered Graduate Student Employees appointment in the immediately following academic term.

No minimum one-year membership period applies to GSU chapter office unless required by controlling law or the governing documents of affiliated organizations.

## Section 4. President

The President shall:

a. serve as chief elected representative of GSU;  
b. preside at GSU membership and Representative Council meetings;  
c. prepare meeting agendas with the Secretary and Vice President;  
d. serve as principal GSU liaison to the existing Local 6350 leadership;  
e. serve as primary liaison to AFT representatives and University counterparts where appropriate;  
f. participate in bargaining and contract administration; and  
g. communicate Local Executive Board actions relevant to GSU.  
i. ensure that decisions of the GSU membership and Representative Council are carried out; and  
j. perform other duties consistent with these bylaws.

## Section 5. Vice President

The Vice President shall:

a. perform the President's duties in the President's absence;  
b. succeed to President when that office becomes vacant;  
c. coordinate the work of GSU committees and caucuses;  
d. assist college/school representatives;  
e. participate in bargaining, organizing, and contract-enforcement work; and  
f. perform other duties assigned by the membership or Representative Council.

## Section 6. Treasurer

The Treasurer shall:

a. maintain records of GSU's approved budget or allocation;  
b. coordinate with the Local 6350 Treasurer regarding expenditures, reimbursements, and financial reporting;  
c. present a financial report at regular Representative Council meetings when financial activity has occurred and at least once each semester;  
d. maintain supporting documentation for GSU expenditures;  
e. assist in preparation of proposed GSU budgets; and  
f. exercise no independent authority over Local 6350 bank accounts except as formally authorized.

## Section 7. Secretary

The Secretary shall:

a. record minutes of GSU membership and Representative Council meetings;  
b. issue required meeting and election notices;  
c. maintain the official copy of GSU bylaws and standing rules;  
d. maintain current officer and Representative Council rosters;  
e. preserve election results and governance records;  
f. coordinate membership communications with the President and communications committee; and  
g. assist with annual Representative Council apportionment using available membership records.

# ARTICLE IV — REPRESENTATIVE COUNCIL

## Section 1. Establishment

GSU shall maintain an elected **Representative Council** as its primary representative body between general membership meetings.

## Section 2. Voting membership

The Council consists of:

a. President;  
b. Vice President;  
c. Treasurer;  
d. Secretary;  
e. academic college/school representatives elected under Section 3; and  
f. two at-large representatives elected by all GSU members.

## Section 3. Academic representation

Each UMass Dartmouth college or school containing at least five GSU bargaining-unit members shall receive:

a. one guaranteed representative; and  
b. one additional representative for each additional fifty GSU members assigned to that college or school.

For purposes of this section:

- 5–54 members = 1 representative;
- 55–104 members = 2 representatives;
- 105–154 members = 3 representatives; and
- the same pattern continues for larger constituencies.

A member's primary college or school affiliation shall determine the constituency in which the member votes for academic representatives.

## Section 4. Initial apportionment

If reliable college/school membership data are unavailable for the initial GSU election, the initial election shall provide one representative for each recognized college or school known to contain GSU bargaining-unit members.

The proportional formula in Section 3 shall begin at the first regular election for which reliable apportionment data are available.

## Section 5. Members in small or unclassified academic units

Members in an academic unit with fewer than five GSU members, or whose membership records do not identify a qualifying college/school constituency, remain entitled to vote for:

- GSU officers;
- at-large representatives; and
- all GSU-wide membership matters.

The Representative Council may establish a temporary combined or at-large constituency for such members by standing rule, provided each affected member has only one academic-representative vote.

## Section 6. Duties of representatives

Representatives shall:

a. communicate regularly with members of their constituency;  
b. bring constituency concerns to the Representative Council;  
c. distribute authorized GSU communications;  
d. help identify workplace and contract-enforcement concerns;  
e. direct members to appropriate grievance or union resources;  
f. support GSU elections, organizing, and bargaining activities;  
g. become familiar with the collective bargaining agreement and GSU governance documents; and  
h. report Council actions back to their constituency.

Representatives are not required to independently prosecute formal grievances without appropriate training and authority.

## Section 7. Powers of the Council

Subject to decisions reserved to the full membership, the Council may:

a. carry out policies adopted by the GSU membership;  
b. adopt standing rules consistent with these bylaws;  
c. establish committees and caucuses;  
d. coordinate organizing and communications;  
e. make recommendations on bargaining priorities;  
f. approve GSU proposals to the Local Executive Board;  
g. approve temporary appointments where permitted by these bylaws;  
h. approve proposed GSU budgets for submission through Local 6350 procedures; and  
i. act on urgent GSU matters between membership meetings when delay would materially impair GSU interests.

## Section 8. Limits

The Council may not:

a. ratify a collective bargaining agreement on behalf of the membership;  
b. amend these bylaws except as provided in Article XIII;  
c. remove an elected GSU officer except through the recall procedures of Article VI;  
d. obligate Local 6350 funds beyond authority delegated under the Local Constitution or approved budget; or  
e. take an action contrary to the Local Constitution, collective bargaining agreement, applicable law, or governing documents of affiliated organizations.

## Section 9. Council quorum

A majority of filled voting Council seats constitutes a quorum.

Unless these bylaws require a greater vote, Council action requires a majority of votes cast while a quorum is present.

# ARTICLE V — ELECTIONS

## Section 1. Regular elections

Regular GSU elections shall be conducted annually during the spring academic semester.

The election schedule should permit a reasonable transition before July 1.

## Section 2. Election Committee

The Representative Council shall establish an Election Committee responsible for administering elections.

No candidate may administer, count, certify, or make discretionary decisions concerning an election in which that candidate appears on the ballot.

If necessary, the Local 6350 Nomination-Election Committee may administer or supervise a GSU election.

## Section 3. Election notice

For an election of GSU officers, written notice of the election shall be mailed to every GSU member at the member's last known home address at least fifteen calendar days before voting closes. The Election Committee may provide additional notice electronically or by other means. Representative Council elections shall use the same notice standard unless controlling requirements permit a different procedure.

The notice shall state:

- offices/seats to be filled;
- eligibility requirements;
- nomination procedure and deadline;
- voting dates;
- voting method; and
- how members may obtain election information.

## Section 4. Nominations

Nominations shall be open to all eligible members.

A member may nominate themself.

The Election Committee shall provide a reasonable nomination period and may establish a standard candidate-acceptance form.

No nomination petition signature requirement shall be imposed for the initial GSU election.

A future standing rule may establish a reasonable petition requirement only if approved by the GSU membership.

## Section 5. Voting

Voting shall be by secret ballot when required by law, the Local Constitution, or affiliated-organization rules.

Electronic voting may be used if the method:

- verifies voter eligibility;
- prevents duplicate voting;
- preserves ballot secrecy;
- creates an auditable result;
- gives eligible members a reasonable opportunity to vote; and
- provides candidates meaningful observer access to the stages of the process necessary to verify election integrity.

No proxy voting is permitted in an officer election.

## Section 6. Electorates

All GSU members vote for:

- President;
- Vice President;
- Treasurer;
- Secretary; and
- at-large representatives.

Only members of an academic constituency vote for that constituency's academic representative(s).

## Section 7. Election standard

The candidate receiving the greatest number of votes for Vice President, Treasurer, Secretary, academic representative, or at-large representative is elected.

A candidate for President must receive a majority of valid votes cast for President.

If no presidential candidate receives a majority, a runoff shall be held between the two candidates receiving the most votes.

## Section 8. Uncontested elections

If only one eligible candidate is nominated for a position and applicable law does not require a contested ballot, the candidate may be declared elected without a ballot after nominations close.

The Election Committee shall document and publish the result.

## Section 9. Candidate observers

Each bona fide candidate shall have the right to designate an observer at each polling location and at each place or stage where ballots are received, eligibility is verified, ballots are counted, or electronic results are processed, to the extent necessary to provide meaningful observation without compromising ballot secrecy.

## Section 10. Results and records

Election results shall be promptly reported to the membership.

Election records, including membership and eligibility lists, nomination and election notices, voting instructions, ballots and ballot records, challenged or voided ballots, tally records, and other records pertaining to the election, shall be preserved for at least one year or for any longer period required by controlling rules.

## Section 11. Challenges

An election challenge shall be submitted in writing to the Election Committee within seven calendar days after results are announced.

The challenger shall identify the alleged irregularity and requested remedy.

A candidate involved in the challenge shall not decide the challenge.

Appeal may be made to the Local 6350 Executive Board or other body required by controlling union rules.

# ARTICLE VI — VACANCIES, SUCCESSION, AND RECALL

## Section 1. Presidential vacancy

If the President resigns, becomes ineligible, or is removed:

a. the Vice President immediately becomes President for the remainder of the term; and  
b. if authorized by the Local Constitution, becomes principal GSU liaison to Local 6350.

## Section 2. Other officer vacancies

If Vice President, Treasurer, or Secretary becomes vacant with more than ninety days remaining in the term, a special election shall be conducted as soon as practicable.

If ninety days or fewer remain, the Representative Council may appoint an eligible member by two-thirds vote to serve the remainder of the term.

## Section 3. Representative vacancies

A vacant academic representative seat with more than ninety days remaining shall normally be filled by special election of the affected constituency.

A vacant at-large seat with more than ninety days remaining shall normally be filled by special GSU-wide election.

If ninety days or fewer remain, or an election is impracticable during an academic recess, the Council may appoint an eligible member by two-thirds vote for the remainder of the term.

## Section 4. Temporary acting appointments

Where immediate performance of an officer's duties is necessary pending a special election, the Council may designate an eligible acting officer by two-thirds vote.

An acting appointment ends when the elected successor takes office.

## Section 5. Recall petition

An elected GSU officer may be subjected to a recall election upon:

a. a petition signed by at least ten percent of GSU members in good standing; or  
b. a two-thirds vote of the Representative Council requesting a recall election.

A constituency representative may be subjected to recall upon a petition signed by at least twenty percent of members of that representative's constituency.

## Section 6. Recall procedure

The member subject to recall shall:

- receive written notice of the grounds stated in the petition or Council action;
- receive a reasonable opportunity to respond to the membership before voting; and
- remain in office until the recall vote is certified unless temporary removal is required by controlling union procedures.

The recall vote shall be by secret ballot after at least fourteen calendar days' notice.

An officer is recalled by two-thirds of valid votes cast in the applicable GSU-wide recall election.

A constituency representative is recalled by a majority of valid votes cast in that constituency.

Any recall process remains subject to due-process requirements in the Local Constitution and affiliated-organization rules.

# ARTICLE VII — MEETINGS

## Section 1. General membership meetings

GSU shall hold at least two general membership meetings during each academic year, ordinarily at least one in the fall and one in the spring.

Additional meetings are encouraged when bargaining, major contract-enforcement issues, elections, or other significant matters require broader membership participation.

## Section 2. Representative Council meetings

The Representative Council shall ordinarily meet at least once each month during the fall and spring semesters.

## Section 3. Special membership meetings

A special GSU membership meeting may be called by:

a. the President;  
b. a majority of the Representative Council; or  
c. a written petition signed by ten percent of GSU membership or twenty members, whichever is fewer.

## Section 4. Notice

Regular GSU membership meetings shall normally receive at least seven calendar days' notice.

Special meetings shall receive as much notice as reasonably practicable and ordinarily not fewer than two calendar days unless an emergency requires shorter notice.

Any vote requiring a longer notice period under these bylaws, the Local Constitution, or law shall comply with the longer period.

## Section 5. Membership-meeting quorum

A quorum for a GSU membership meeting shall be:

- ten percent of GSU membership;
- but not fewer than ten members when GSU has at least ten members; and
- not more than twenty-five members.

Members participating through an authorized electronic or hybrid meeting platform count toward quorum.

## Section 6. Voting

Unless a greater vote is required, action at a membership meeting requires a majority of votes cast while quorum is present.

Voting may occur outside a live meeting when these bylaws expressly authorize a ballot or when the Representative Council submits a question to the full membership using a secure voting process.

## Section 7. Electronic and hybrid meetings

GSU membership, Representative Council, and committee meetings may be held in person, electronically, or in hybrid form.

The meeting method must permit participating members to hear or read the proceedings substantially contemporaneously and must protect the integrity of any vote.

---

# ARTICLE VIII — COMMITTEES AND CAUCUSES

## Section 1. Authority

The Representative Council may establish standing or temporary committees and caucuses.

## Section 2. Cross-cutting groups

GSU should maintain or establish committees/caucuses as membership need warrants, including:

- Research Employees / RAs;
- Teaching and Instructional Employees / TAs;
- Master's Student Employees;
- Doctoral Student Employees;
- International Graduate Employees;
- Organizing & Membership;
- Contract Enforcement / Grievances;
- Communications; and
- Bargaining.

## Section 3. Participation

Committees and caucuses should be open to GSU members affected by or interested in their work unless confidentiality, grievance privacy, bargaining strategy, or another legitimate union purpose requires restricted participation.

## Section 4. Chairs

Unless otherwise provided by these bylaws, a committee may elect its own chair or the Representative Council may designate an initial convener.

Committee chairs are not members of the Local 6350 Executive Board solely by virtue of the GSU committee position.

## Section 5. Reports

Committees shall report significant recommendations and actions to the Representative Council.

---

# ARTICLE IX — COLLECTIVE BARGAINING

## Section 1. GSU Bargaining Committee

GSU shall maintain or establish a Bargaining Committee when collective bargaining or bargaining preparation is required.

## Section 2. Selection

The President and Vice President shall serve ex officio on the Bargaining Committee.

Before substantive negotiations begin, the Representative Council shall recommend:

- the total size of the bargaining committee;
- any constituency or employment-type representation requirements; and
- a nomination and selection process for additional members.

The final bargaining-team structure shall be presented to the GSU membership for approval unless an urgent bargaining obligation requires an interim team.

## Section 3. Member input

The Bargaining Committee shall create meaningful opportunities for members to identify and prioritize bargaining issues, which may include:

- surveys;
- listening sessions;
- constituency meetings;
- bargaining updates; and
- membership meetings.

## Section 4. Authority

The Bargaining Committee represents the Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit in negotiations subject to the Local 6350 Constitution and any legal or affiliation obligations of the Local.

No tentative agreement becomes a ratified collective bargaining agreement solely by action of the Bargaining Committee.

## Section 5. Tentative agreements

The Bargaining Committee shall provide members a written summary of a tentative agreement before ratification consistent with Local 6350 Article XV.

## Section 6. Ratification

Only eligible members of the Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit may vote to ratify a collective bargaining agreement covering that bargaining unit.

Ratification procedures shall comply with the Local Constitution.

---

# ARTICLE X — GRIEVANCES AND CONTRACT ADMINISTRATION

## Section 1. Responsibility

GSU officers and trained or designated representatives shall work with Local 6350's grievance structure to enforce the Graduate Student Employees collective bargaining agreement.

## Section 2. Representatives as first contact

Academic and at-large representatives should be prepared to:

- identify possible contract issues;
- provide basic information;
- document member concerns;
- refer members to trained grievance representatives; and
- follow up on systemic issues affecting their constituency.

## Section 3. Confidentiality

Grievance, disciplinary, accommodation, immigration, personnel, and other sensitive member matters shall be handled only by persons with a legitimate union need to know.

Representative Council meetings shall not publicly disclose confidential member information.

## Section 4. Systemic issues

Patterns of contract violations or recurring workplace concerns may be reported to the Representative Council in anonymized or appropriately confidential form so GSU can determine whether broader organizing, bargaining, or enforcement action is warranted.

---

# ARTICLE XI — FINANCES

## Section 1. Relationship to Local treasury

GSU funds are part of or administered through Local 6350 unless the Local Constitution or Executive Board expressly establishes another lawful arrangement.

## Section 2. Budget

The Treasurer shall prepare a proposed GSU budget with the President for Representative Council review.

The approved proposal shall be submitted through Local 6350's budgeting process.

## Section 3. Expenditures

GSU expenditures must:

- fall within an approved GSU/Local budget or receive required Local authorization;
- be documented;
- comply with Local financial controls; and
- be reported by the GSU Treasurer.

## Section 4. No unilateral account authority

No GSU officer may independently open a bank account in the name of GSU or Local 6350, incur debt in the name of the union, or bind Local 6350 financially without authorization.

## Section 5. Financial transparency

The Treasurer shall provide GSU members reasonable access to non-confidential GSU budget and expenditure reports.

---

# ARTICLE XII — RECORDS AND COMMUNICATIONS

## Section 1. Governance records

GSU shall maintain:

- current bylaws and standing rules;
- officer and Representative Council rosters;
- approved meeting minutes;
- election notices, results, and required records;
- committee charters where applicable; and
- non-confidential financial reports.

## Section 2. Union-controlled systems

To the extent practicable, official GSU records, websites, domains, social accounts, and administrative credentials shall be maintained in union-controlled systems with access held by more than one current officer.

## Section 3. Officer transition

Outgoing officers shall promptly transfer union records, credentials, files, and other GSU-controlled property to their successors.

No officer may treat official GSU records, accounts, websites, or credentials as personal property.

## Section 4. Privacy

Membership lists, grievance information, private contact information, bargaining strategy, and other confidential union information shall not be published or disclosed except as authorized for legitimate union purposes.

---

# ARTICLE XIII — AMENDMENTS TO THESE BYLAWS

## Section 1. Proposal

An amendment to these bylaws may be proposed by:

a. a majority vote of the Representative Council; or  
b. a petition signed by at least ten percent of GSU members.

## Section 2. Notice

The proposed amendment shall be distributed to GSU members at least fourteen calendar days before voting closes.

## Section 3. Adoption

An amendment is adopted by two-thirds of valid votes cast by GSU members, provided the amendment does not conflict with the Local 6350 Constitution or controlling requirements.

## Section 4. Conforming review

Adopted amendments shall be provided to the Local 6350 Secretary and President for the Local's records and for review of consistency with the Local Constitution and affiliated-organization requirements.

A conforming review does not authorize alteration of a GSU bylaw without the amendment process in this Article unless a provision is unenforceable because it conflicts with controlling law or superior union governing documents.

---

# ARTICLE XIV — RULES OF ORDER

Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised, shall govern GSU meetings in matters not addressed by:

1. the Local 6350 Constitution;
2. these bylaws;
3. adopted GSU standing rules; or
4. applicable controlling requirements.

The chair may use reasonable simplified meeting procedures when doing so preserves member participation and does not conflict with a required parliamentary rule.

---

# ARTICLE XV — INITIAL TRANSITION

## Section 1. Effectiveness

These bylaws become effective only after:

a. Local 6350 adopts constitutional authority sufficient to recognize the GSU chapter and its governance; and  
b. the GSU membership adopts these bylaws through the authorized process.

## Section 2. Initial Election Committee

For the first election, the existing GSU leadership group and the Local 6350 Nomination-Election Committee shall establish a neutral election-administration process consistent with controlling requirements.

No candidate may administer the portion of the election in which that candidate appears on the ballot.

## Section 3. Initial nominations

The initial nomination period should open within fourteen days after the necessary constitutional authority takes effect, unless a different schedule is required for compliance or administration.

## Section 4. Initial election

The initial election shall occur as soon as practicable after the required member notice.

## Section 5. Initial terms

Initial GSU officers and representatives take office upon certification of the initial election.

Their initial terms expire June 30, 2027.

Regular annual terms begin July 1, 2027.

## Section 6. Interim leadership

Existing informal or organizing leadership may continue only as necessary to conduct ordinary GSU work and facilitate the first election.

Interim status does not convert an unelected position into an elected constitutional office.
