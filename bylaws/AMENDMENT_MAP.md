# Local 6350 GSU Amendment Map

Status: working analysis, not proposed amendment text  
Source baseline: `bylaws/source/Bylaw-Edits.md` (Local 6350 Constitution, amended through April 2022)  
Target vote discussed in leadership comments: October 21, 2026

## Purpose

This document identifies where the current Local 6350 Constitution must or may need to change in order to formally incorporate the Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit (GSU / Unit B), create democratic GSU governance, and preserve appropriate bargaining-unit autonomy.

This is an amendment map, not final legal language. Final language should be reviewed against AFT/AFT Massachusetts requirements and the Local's charter before circulation.

## Working structural model

Local 6350 should be treated as the common parent local containing distinct bargaining units rather than treating GSU as a committee subordinate to the Maintainers.

Working model:

- Local 6350
  - Maintainers bargaining unit / chapter
  - Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit / chapter (GSU / Unit B)
- Local-wide constitutional officers and Executive Board
- Unit-specific elected leadership
- GSU Representative Council for graduate-worker representation
- Unit-specific bargaining, grievance, and contract-ratification authority where appropriate
- Local-wide governance for matters affecting the Federation as a whole

The current Constitution already contains two pieces of language compatible with this model:
1. Article IX, Section 5 refers to members voting for "chapter chairpersons of their respective chapters."
2. Article XV limits contract-ratification voting to members of the bargaining unit covered by the proposed agreement.

## Priority legend

- **REQUIRED** — current language conflicts with or fails to recognize GSU.
- **STRUCTURAL** — needed if the proposed two-unit governance model is adopted.
- **RECOMMENDED** — modernization or clarification strongly worth doing now.
- **PRESERVE / MINOR** — no major GSU-specific change appears necessary.
- **OPEN QUESTION** — requires confirmation from Local/AFT leadership or comparator bylaws before drafting.

---

## Article I — Name

**Current effect:** Names the organization "U-Mass Dartmouth Maintainers, Local 6350, AFT Massachusetts, AFL-CIO."

**Classification:** OPEN QUESTION / likely REQUIRED.

**Issue:** The constitutional name identifies the entire Local with the Maintainers even though Local 6350 now contains a graduate-student bargaining unit.

**Drafting direction:**
- Confirm the exact chartered/legal name of Local 6350 with AFT Massachusetts.
- If the charter permits, consider a broader Local-wide name and preserve "Maintainers" as the Unit A / Maintainers chapter identity.
- If the legal name must remain unchanged, add explicit constituent-unit language elsewhere so the Constitution unambiguously covers GSU.

**Do not change the legal name until charter status is confirmed.**

---

## Article II — Objectives

**Current effect:** States broad labor, civil-rights, university-democracy, and student-welfare objectives.

**Classification:** PRESERVE / MINOR.

**Issue:** Nothing here expressly prevents GSU representation, although some wording is dated and Maintainer-oriented.

**Drafting direction:**
- Preserve unless leadership wants a modernization pass.
- Optional: use more inclusive references to employees/workers and the bargaining units represented by the Local.
- Avoid spending the October amendment window on stylistic rewrites unless they affect GSU governance.

---

## Article III — Membership

**Current effect:** Membership may include maintenance and other University-related personnel, but Section 1 excludes "all other organized units of the AFT."

**Classification:** REQUIRED.

**Issue:** This is the clearest direct conflict with incorporating GSU.

**Drafting direction:**
- Define membership to include employees represented by every bargaining unit within Local 6350, including Graduate Student Employees.
- Define the Local's constituent bargaining units/chapters.
- Establish that a member belongs to the chapter/unit corresponding to the bargaining unit in which they are employed.
- Clarify treatment of workers who change classifications or bargaining units.
- Preserve due-process provisions for membership discipline unless AFT review identifies a required update.

**Open questions:**
- Exact legal description/name of Unit B.
- Whether the Constitution should name existing units individually or define a mechanism for adding future units.
- Whether graduate workers may retain membership between qualifying appointments and, if so, under what conditions.

---

## Article IV — Officers & Duties of Officers

**Current effect:** The Federation elects President, Vice-President, Treasurer, and Secretary triennially. One year of good-standing membership is required to hold office.

**Classification:** REQUIRED / STRUCTURAL.

**Issue:** The Constitution does not distinguish Local-wide officers from bargaining-unit officers and the one-year eligibility rule may be impractical for a newly organized, high-turnover graduate-worker unit.

**Drafting direction:**
- Preserve clearly identified Local-wide constitutional officers.
- Create elected bargaining-unit leadership rather than making GSU leadership an appointed committee chair.
- Evaluate a structure using bargaining-unit Vice Presidents or bargaining-unit Chairs.
- Define which GSU officer(s) automatically sit on the Local Executive Board.
- Create a transition exception so GSU members can hold office before one full year of Local membership has elapsed.
- Define succession and authority of bargaining-unit officers.

**GSU internal structure under consideration:**
- President / Chair
- Vice President
- Treasurer
- Secretary
- Representative Council elected from academic constituencies

**Open questions:**
- Should all four GSU officers be constitutional Local officers, or should only the GSU President/Unit VP be a Local officer while the others are GSU chapter officers?
- Does AFT prefer the "Bargaining Unit Vice-President" model used by other multi-unit locals?
- What term length best fits graduate-worker turnover?

---

## Article V — Committees

**Current effect:** Local committees are appointed by the President. The Collective Bargaining Committee is built around Maintainers classifications: Custodial, Grounds, Power Plant, and Trades.

**Classification:** REQUIRED / STRUCTURAL.

**Issue:** The bargaining structure is Maintainers-specific, and appointed committee leadership cannot substitute for democratically elected GSU leadership.

**Drafting direction:**
- Distinguish Local-wide committees from unit-specific committees.
- Preserve existing Maintainers bargaining representation for the Maintainers unit.
- Establish a GSU bargaining committee selected by and accountable to the GSU bargaining unit.
- Clarify that appointed committee provisions do not override elected unit offices.
- Consider adding an Organizing/Membership committee structure.

**Representative Council:** Consider adding a separate article rather than treating the GSU Representative Council as an ordinary appointed committee.

---

## Proposed New Article — GSU Representative Council / Bargaining-Unit Chapters

**Classification:** STRUCTURAL.

A new article may be cleaner than overloading Articles IV and V.

**Possible functions:**
- Formally establish constituent bargaining-unit chapters.
- Recognize GSU / Unit B as a chapter of Local 6350.
- Give each chapter authority over internal matters unique to its bargaining unit, subject to the Local Constitution and AFT requirements.
- Establish the GSU Representative Council.
- Define electoral constituencies and powers.
- Permit standing caucuses/committees without turning overlapping identities into separate voting districts.

**Working GSU representation model:**
- Academic college/school representation as the primary elected constituency.
- At-large seats if needed to prevent underrepresentation.
- Cross-cutting committees/caucuses for:
  - Research employees / RAs
  - Teaching/instructional employees / TAs
  - Master's students
  - Doctoral students
  - International graduate employees
  - Organizing/membership
  - Contract enforcement/grievances
  - Communications

**Reasoning:** College/school constituencies are comparatively stable; RA/TA, MS/PhD, and international-status interests overlap and are better handled through committees/caucuses than multiple overlapping electoral districts.

---

## Article VI — Duties of Committees

**Current effect:** Defines duties of the Local's standing committees.

**Classification:** STRUCTURAL / RECOMMENDED.

**Issue:** If unit-specific committees are created in Article V, their authority must be defined here or in the new bargaining-unit/chapter article.

**Drafting direction:**
- Clarify the respective roles of Local-wide and unit-specific grievance/bargaining committees.
- Give the affected bargaining unit control over development of its bargaining proposals.
- Define coordination requirements where an issue affects more than one bargaining unit.
- Avoid giving one unit's leadership unilateral authority over the other unit's contract administration.

---

## Article VII — Executive Board

**Current effect:** Executive Board consists of all elected officers, committee chairs, and the chief delegate to the local labor council.

**Classification:** REQUIRED / STRUCTURAL.

**Issue:** "All elected officers" becomes ambiguous once GSU creates multiple elected representatives. Automatically seating every GSU college representative on the Local Executive Board may make the Board unintentionally large.

**Drafting direction:**
- Enumerate the Executive Board composition rather than relying only on "all elected officers."
- Guarantee meaningful GSU representation.
- Specify which bargaining-unit officers have voice and vote.
- Preserve a workable quorum after expansion.
- Consider balanced or proportional representation while ensuring each bargaining unit has a guaranteed seat.

**Open question:** Whether GSU should have one bargaining-unit VP/Chair on the Executive Board or multiple GSU officers.

---

## Article VIII — Affiliations

**Current effect:** Governs delegates to AFT, AFT Massachusetts, state and regional labor councils.

**Classification:** PRESERVE / MINOR.

**Issue:** Delegate eligibility currently incorporates the officer-eligibility standard, which includes the one-year rule.

**Drafting direction:**
- If Article IV/IX changes the one-year requirement, make Article VIII consistent.
- Confirm that delegate allocation/election does not unintentionally exclude newly organized GSU members.
- Otherwise preserve.

---

## Article IX — Elections

**Current effect:** Establishes Local election procedures; requires one year of membership; provides triennial July elections; refers to chapter chairpersons; fills most vacancies by Executive Board appointment.

**Classification:** REQUIRED / STRUCTURAL.

**Issue:** This article contains useful chapter language but does not define the chapters. The election cycle and vacancy process may be poorly suited to graduate-worker turnover.

**Drafting direction:**
- Define who elects Local-wide officers.
- Define that bargaining-unit/chapter officers are elected only by members of that unit/chapter.
- Preserve secret-ballot and candidate-observer protections.
- Create a transition election for the initial GSU leadership.
- Add special-election procedures for vacancies where appropriate.
- Create a GSU eligibility exception or alternative eligibility standard to the one-year rule.
- Decide whether GSU elections occur annually, biennially, or on another cycle compatible with graduate appointments.
- Preserve records for the legally/AFT-required period.

**Important existing language:** Section 5 already says only chapter members vote for chapter chairpersons of their respective chapters. This should be clarified and used rather than deleted unless AFT advises otherwise.

---

## Article X — Meetings

**Current effect:** Local membership meets quarterly; special meetings may be called under specified procedures; quorum is 20% of membership.

**Classification:** STRUCTURAL / RECOMMENDED.

**Issue:** The Constitution needs to distinguish Local-wide meetings from bargaining-unit/chapter meetings.

**Drafting direction:**
- Preserve Local-wide regular meetings.
- Authorize each bargaining-unit chapter to hold its own membership meetings.
- Set unit-level quorum or authorize chapter bylaws to do so.
- Permit remote/hybrid notice and participation if AFT/legal requirements allow.
- Clarify who can call a GSU special meeting.

**Open question:** Whether the 20% Local-wide quorum remains workable after adding the graduate bargaining unit.

---

## Article XI — Finances

**Current effect:** Local dues are tied partly to six hours of pay at Maintainers Salary Schedule Grade 9, Step I; creates a militancy/defense fund; establishes a "U-Mass Dartmouth Maintainers Scholarship Award."

**Classification:** REQUIRED / OPEN QUESTION.

**Issue:** The dues formula and scholarship are expressly Maintainers-specific.

**Drafting direction:**
- Confirm dues obligations and per-capita structure for GSU with the Treasurer/AFT Massachusetts before drafting.
- Determine whether Local funds are pooled, allocated by unit, or partly unit-controlled.
- Preserve the existing Maintainers scholarship as a Maintainers program if desired rather than silently making it a GSU obligation.
- Define authority for unit budgets/expenditures if GSU will maintain a unit budget.
- Establish transparent reporting across units.

---

## Article XII — Rules of Order

**Current effect:** Robert's Rules governs matters not covered by the Constitution/bylaws.

**Classification:** PRESERVE / MINOR.

**Drafting direction:**
- Preserve.
- Clarify that approved chapter bylaws/standing rules govern chapter-specific matters before defaulting to Robert's Rules.

---

## Article XIII — Amendments to the Constitution

**Current effect:** Amendments require a two-thirds vote of members present at a regular meeting, provided the proposal was read at the prior regular meeting or distributed to all members at least 15 days before the vote.

**Classification:** PRESERVE / PROCEDURAL CRITICAL.

**October 21, 2026 target:** If the Local uses the 15-day distribution route, the amendment text must be presented to all members no later than **October 6, 2026**.

**Working internal deadline:** Complete leadership/AFT review and circulate before October 6 rather than using the last permissible day.

**Drafting direction:**
- No GSU-specific substantive change is necessarily required for the October package.
- Consider later clarifying whether amendments affecting only internal chapter bylaws may be adopted by the affected chapter, subject to consistency with the Local Constitution and AFT rules.

---

## Article XIV — Availability of the Constitution

**Current effect:** Requires copies to be submitted to AFT and AFT Massachusetts and made available to members.

**Classification:** PRESERVE / RECOMMENDED MODERNIZATION.

**Drafting direction:**
- Preserve submission requirements.
- Add electronic publication/access if desired.
- Maintain an authoritative current version and amendment history in union-controlled storage.

---

## Article XV — Ratification of Contracts

**Current effect:** Ratification is conducted by members of the bargaining unit covered by the proposed agreement.

**Classification:** PRESERVE / IMPORTANT FOUNDATION.

**Issue:** This is already the correct multi-unit principle.

**Drafting direction:**
- Preserve the bargaining-unit-only voting rule.
- Clarify which unit leadership calls the ratification meeting and administers the vote.
- Consider adding procedures for electronic/secure secret-ballot voting if permitted.
- Do not give members of one bargaining unit a vote on the other unit's CBA.

---

## Article XVI — Order of Business at Regular Meetings

**Current effect:** Sets a standard agenda for Local membership meetings.

**Classification:** MINOR / RECOMMENDED.

**Drafting direction:**
- Add bargaining-unit/chapter reports if useful.
- Add Representative Council report if the Council is created.
- Keep detailed GSU meeting agendas in chapter bylaws/standing rules rather than overloading the Local Constitution.

---

# Cross-article decisions required before final redline

## 1. Legal/chartered Local name
Confirm whether "U-Mass Dartmouth Maintainers, Local 6350" is the AFT-chartered name that must remain.

## 2. Formal names of bargaining units
Confirm the official titles of the Maintainers bargaining unit and Graduate Student Employees bargaining unit / Unit B.

## 3. Local-wide vs unit-specific officers
Decide whether the preferred model is:
- Local President + Local VP + Treasurer + Secretary + elected bargaining-unit VPs; or
- existing Local officers + elected chapter Presidents/Chairs; or
- another AFT-approved multi-unit structure.

## 4. GSU officer structure
Working proposal:
- GSU President/Chair
- GSU Vice President
- GSU Treasurer
- GSU Secretary

Need to decide which of these are Local constitutional officers and which are chapter officers.

## 5. Representative Council
Define:
- represented colleges/schools
- allocation of seats
- at-large seats
- eligibility
- terms
- vacancies
- powers
- relationship to GSU officers and GSU membership meetings

## 6. Election timing and eligibility
Graduate-worker terms and turnover likely justify a shorter election cycle and a specific exception to the one-year membership rule.

## 7. Bargaining and grievance autonomy
Define what GSU controls independently and what remains Local-wide.

## 8. Finances
Obtain Treasurer/AFT guidance on dues allocation, unit budgets, Local funds, and signing authority.

## 9. Transition provisions
The amendment package should include a one-time transition mechanism so GSU can hold initial elections promptly rather than waiting for the ordinary triennial July cycle.

# Proposed drafting sequence

1. Confirm chartered Local name and formal bargaining-unit names.
2. Obtain comparator bylaws and AFT guidance.
3. Decide Local-wide / bargaining-unit officer architecture.
4. Decide GSU Representative Council seat structure.
5. Resolve election term, eligibility, and transition rules.
6. Resolve unit bargaining/grievance authority.
7. Resolve finances.
8. Draft the constitutional redline.
9. Draft concise GSU chapter bylaws/standing rules for internal governance.
10. Complete leadership/AFT review.
11. Distribute final amendment package before the Article XIII deadline.
